# Product Brief

**Problem.** Teams lose the thread between what was decided and what was built:
requirements drift silently after upstream documents change.

**User.** A small product/engineering team that keeps its specs in the repo.

**Outcome.** Every downstream artifact declares what it derives from; when an
upstream changes, the stale dependents are flagged until a human reconciles them.
